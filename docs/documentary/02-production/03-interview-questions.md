# Interview Questions Template

## Vision / Product Owner

1. What problem were you trying to solve?
2. Why did existing approaches fail users?
3. What outcome did you optimize for?

## Engineer / Implementer

1. What was technically hardest?
2. What changed after iteration?
3. What evidence proves the solution works?

## User / Playtester

1. What felt better after the change?
2. What was easier to understand or use?
3. What still needs improvement?

## Soundbite Targets

- Problem in plain language
- Turning point in the build journey
- Practical user takeaway
